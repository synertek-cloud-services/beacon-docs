Graphical remote-desktop access is not a Beacon beta capability. Beacon currently provides browser-based Remote Shell through its outbound session relay.

Beacon does not integrate, deploy, configure, or relay RustDesk. If your organization needs graphical remote control, use an independently approved remote-access product and its own security, consent, and logging controls. Do not rely on old instructions that describe a Beacon RustDesk workflow.

For supported remote operation, see [Use Remote Shell](/kb/remote-access/connecting-via-rustdesk/).
